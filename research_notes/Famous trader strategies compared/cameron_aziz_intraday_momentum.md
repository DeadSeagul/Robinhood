## Q1. Ross Cameron: the mechanical rules of his small-cap momentum setups (Gap and Go, bull flag, micro pullback, ABCD, flat top)

### Takeaway
Cameron's rules are stated on Warrior Trading's site, and parts of them can be coded. The **stock filter** is: up ≥10% on the day, relative volume ≥5x, price under $20 (sweet spot $2-8), float under 20M shares, and a fresh news catalyst. The **entry** is "the first candle to make a new high" after a 2-3 candle pullback, or a break of the pre-market high or the high of the first 1-minute candle at 9:30. The **stop** is the low of the pullback or of that candle, with a minimum 2:1 profit-to-loss ratio, and he trades mostly 9:30-11:30 ET on 10-second, 1-minute and 5-minute charts. Several exits are discretionary: Level 2/tape, "breakout or bailout", and selling into "extension bars". Some thresholds also changed over time; older pages use float under 100M and RVOL 2x.

### Cited Findings

**Stock-selection criteria (the "5 criteria"; later called "pillars" by third parties)**
- [Educator claim] "The first thing I look for is that a stock must be up at least 10% versus the prior day's close in order to consider trading it. When I look at my metrics they prove this is where I make the most money." — [Warrior Trading, "Best Stocks to Day Trade" watch-list page (accessed Oct 2026)](https://www.warriortrading.com/day-trading-watch-list-top-stocks-to-watch/)
- [Educator claim] Relative volume: "Relative Volume is defined by the average volume over the last 14 days. So if a stock has 5x higher volume today, its Relative Volume is 5. I want to trade stocks with a Relative Volume of at least 5, but higher is better… Trading stocks with 2x or 3x Relative Volume doesn't produce nearly as much profit as 5x and higher." — [Warrior watch-list page](https://www.warriortrading.com/day-trading-watch-list-top-stocks-to-watch/)
- [Educator claim] Price: "I prefer stocks under $20/share… if a stock is priced at 20 or 30 cents, I'll probably leave it alone… I typically find the sweet spot is between $2-8 per share," trading "only 5,000-10,000 shares." — [Warrior watch-list page](https://www.warriortrading.com/day-trading-watch-list-top-stocks-to-watch/). His bio says his best early trades were "Typically priced between $2 and $10." — [Warrior, Ross Cameron bio](https://www.warriortrading.com/ross-cameron/)
- [Educator claim] Catalyst: "Once I find a stock that is up at least 10%, has 5x Relative Volume, and has an acceptable price, it's time to search for the news catalyst." Stocks up 100%+ on no news "can be subject to regulatory investigation and can be halted," and "stocks with 'buy out' news rarely provide opportunities." — [Warrior watch-list page](https://www.warriortrading.com/day-trading-watch-list-top-stocks-to-watch/)
- [Educator claim] Float: "With a float of under 20 million shares, a company will be more likely to make larger percentage moves… In total, these 5 criteria create the supply-demand imbalance that I look for each day." — [Warrior watch-list page](https://www.warriortrading.com/day-trading-watch-list-top-stocks-to-watch/)
- [Educator claim] Older, looser version (page first published 2014-08-07, modified 2025-12-12): "Float of under 100mil shares"; "Strong Daily Charts (above the Moving Averages and with no nearby resistance)"; "High Relative Volume of at least 2x above average (This compares the current volume for today to the average volume for this time of day)"; a fundamental catalyst. The entry checklist on the same page says "under 100mil shares, but under 20million shares is ideal." — [Warrior, Momentum Day Trading Strategy](https://www.warriortrading.com/momentum-day-trading-strategy/). A glossary page also says "we like to see RVOL at 2 or higher." — [Warrior, Relative Volume](https://www.warriortrading.com/relative-volume-day-trading-terminology/)
- [Regulator, quoting Cameron] In a July 11, 2020 YouTube video cited by the FTC, Cameron said he had "over $3, I think it was $3.3 million in profit in the last three and a half, four years of trading and almost 98% of it comes from stocks that have relative volume of 5 or higher." — [FTC Complaint ¶42](https://www.ftc.gov/system/files/ftc_gov/pdf/2023198WarriorTradingComplaint_0.pdf)
- [Third party, unofficial] Community scanners that implement "Ross Cameron's 5 pillars" use roughly price $1-$20 or $2-$20, %change ≥10%, RVOL ≥5x, float ≤10-20M, plus a manual news check. These are not official definitions. — [TradingView ideas](https://www.tradingview.com/ideas/ross); [Quantamental Trader Substack](https://quantamentaltrader.substack.com/p/high-float-turnover-trading-manual)

**Gap and Go (page first published 2015-01-11, modified 2025-06-03)**
- [Educator claim] "Gaps of more than 4% are good for Gap and Go trading. Gaps of less than 4% are usually going to be filled." He confirms the catalyst first, and "the Gap and Go Strategy is specifically for trades between 9:30 – 10 AM ET… a profit usually by 10 AM." — [Warrior, Gap and Go](https://www.warriortrading.com/gap-go/)
- [Educator claim] Checklist, verbatim: "1) Scan for all gappers more 4% 2) Hunt for Catalyst… 3) Mark out pre-market highs and high of any pre-market flags 4) Prepare order to buy the pre-market highs once the market opens 5) At 9:30 AM, as soon as the bell rings, I buy the high of the first 1-min candle (1-min opening range breakout) with a stop at the low of that candle or buy the Pre-Market highs." The listed entry setups are "Break of Pre-market flags", "Opening Range Breakouts" and "Red to Green Moves". Setups 4-6 are "for Trading Course Members Only." — [Warrior, Gap and Go](https://www.warriortrading.com/gap-go/)
- [Educator claim] On float turnover: "A stock that has a 10mil share float and trades 1mil share pre-market has already traded 10% of the float… These are the type of stocks that can run 50-100% in one day." — [Warrior, Gap and Go](https://www.warriortrading.com/gap-go/)

**Bull flag ("Updated: October 22, 2023. Written by Ross Cameron")**
- [Educator claim] Structure: a flagpole of "at least one, but sometimes several large green candles" on high relative volume, then consolidation "on lighter volume", then a breakout "on high relative volume." He trades it "primarily… on 5-minute and 1-minute stock charts." — [Warrior, Bull Flag](https://www.warriortrading.com/bull-flag-trading/)
- [Educator claim] Failure signs: "retraces more than 50% of the initial move"; pullback volume higher than the pole volume; a break of "the 9-period Exponential Moving Average or the Volume Weighted Average Price (VWAP)." — [Warrior, Bull Flag](https://www.warriortrading.com/bull-flag-trading/)
- [Educator claim] Entry checklist, verbatim: "The correct entry is the first candle to make a new high after 2-3 red candles of pulling back… The moment a green candle breaks the high is when I'm a buying. The max loss or stop is at the low of the pullback. I need a 2:1 profit vs loss ratio." "If the pullback goes to 4 or 5 candles, I'll begin to feel concerned." — [Warrior, Bull Flag](https://www.warriortrading.com/bull-flag-trading/)
- [Educator claim] Exits: "breakout or bailout… If a stock doesn't immediately move higher after my entry, I exit." "If my first profit target has touched, I sell half… and adjust stop to breakeven for remainder." "If my entry is on the 5min chart, typically I will sell on the first red candle on the 5min chart." Level 2 sellers and time-and-sales selling are also bail-out triggers. — [Warrior, Bull Flag](https://www.warriortrading.com/bull-flag-trading/)
- [Educator claim] Flat-top breakout is the variant where consolidation is "within a few cents of the highs" and "the entry is at the high of day." — [Warrior, Bull Flag](https://www.warriortrading.com/bull-flag-trading/)

**Stops, sizing, time of day, exits (Momentum page, 2014, modified 2025-12-12)**
- [Educator claim] "I usually set a tight stop order just below the first pull back. If the stop is further than 20 cents away, I may decide to stop out minus 20 cents… if I risk 20 cents, it's because I have the potential to make 40 cents." Sizing example: "If I have a 20 cent stop and want to keep my max risk to $500 I'll take 2500 shares." — [Warrior, Momentum Day Trading Strategy](https://www.warriortrading.com/momentum-day-trading-strategy/)
- [Educator claim] "I focus my trading from 9:30am – 11:30am… After 11:30am I prefer to only trade off the 5-min chart. The 1-min chart becomes too choppy." — [Warrior, Momentum Day Trading Strategy](https://www.warriortrading.com/momentum-day-trading-strategy/)
- [Educator claim] Exit indicators: "#1: I will sell 1/2 when I hit my first profit target… I then adjust my stop to my entry price"; "#2: If I haven't already sold 1/2, the first candle to close red is an exit indicator"; "#3: Extension bar… When I'm lucky enough to have a stock spike up while I'm holding, I sell into the spike." — [Warrior, Momentum Day Trading Strategy](https://www.warriortrading.com/momentum-day-trading-strategy/)
- [Educator claim] In the generic version: "I'm buying the moment a green candle breaks over the high of a previous red candle… My max loss is the low of the previous red candle and my profit target is that the stock hits a new high of day." — [Warrior, Day Trading guide](https://www.warriortrading.com/day-trading/)
- [Educator claim] Risk/reward: "If I risk $100, I want to make at least $200… With a 2:1 ratio, I only have to be right 50% of the time"; "If I don't see a clean 2:1 setup, I don't trade"; and for lower-conviction setups he wants "3:1 or 4:1". — [Warrior, Risk/Reward Ratio (modified 2025-11-18)](https://www.warriortrading.com/risk-reward-ratio/)
- [Educator claim] Personal rules: "No trading after two red trades; No revenge trading midday; No oversized positions without A+ setups; Max daily loss limit in place." No numeric max daily loss is given on the page. He uses limit orders only, no market orders. — [Warrior, Day Trading Rules (modified 2026-08-03)](https://www.warriortrading.com/day-trading-rules/)

**Micro pullback and ABCD**
- [Educator claim] Micro pullback: "I'm usually looking at 10-second or 1-minute charts. It's a tiny pause that happens during an explosive breakout. You might only see it as one small red candle or even a doji before the move continues… I often spot these around whole dollar levels — they let me sneak in early with a tight stop." The bull-flag page calls these "mini bull flag patterns" traded on "10-second and 1-minute charts." — [Warrior, Best Day Trading Patterns (modified 2025-11-14)](https://www.warriortrading.com/best-day-trading-patterns/); [Warrior, Bull Flag](https://www.warriortrading.com/bull-flag-trading/)
- [Educator claim] Whole and half-dollar levels: "$1, $2, $5, or $7.50 — act like magnets… I love buying off $2 or $7." — [Warrior, Best Day Trading Patterns](https://www.warriortrading.com/best-day-trading-patterns/)
- [Educator claim] ABCD, version 1: "I'll usually enter near point C with a tight stop or wait for the breakout confirmation at point D if the volume looks strong." The page classes ABCD as a "Reversal or Caution" pattern that "often form[s] when bull flags fail." — [Warrior, Best Day Trading Patterns](https://www.warriortrading.com/best-day-trading-patterns/)
- [Educator claim] ABCD, version 2 (page published 2025-01-21): BC pullback "preferably no more than 61.8%". "The best entry point is typically at the start of the CD leg, just as the price breaks above… point B." "Stop losses should be right below Point C." — [Warrior, ABCD Pattern](https://www.warriortrading.com/abcd-pattern/)

**Stated accuracy and win/loss metrics**
- [Educator claim] "I've taken over 24,268 day trades… Currently, I'm trading with an accuracy of 69%" and "Given my accuracy is about 69% on over 24,000 trades, I'm still wrong 31% of the time." This is on the bull-flag page, updated Oct 22, 2023. — [Warrior, Bull Flag](https://www.warriortrading.com/bull-flag-trading/)
- [Educator claim] Elsewhere: "That type of payoff on winners allows me to stay profitable even if only 65% of my trades pan out." — [Warrior, Risk/Reward Ratio](https://www.warriortrading.com/risk-reward-ratio/)
- [Educator claim] In 2015-era posts he says he focuses "on ONLY trading setups I have 80-90% conviction in." — [Warrior, Momentum Trading blog (modified 2025-03-25)](https://www.warriortrading.com/momentum-trading/)

### Inferences
- [Inference] The most mechanical Cameron rules are: (a) the **1-minute opening-range breakout** on a ≥4% gapper (buy above the high of the first 1-minute bar, stop at its low); (b) the **pre-market-high breakout**; (c) the **bull-flag "first candle to make a new high"** (after 2-3 down bars with retrace ≤50% and price above VWAP and 9 EMA, buy a break of the prior bar's high, stop at the pullback low, sell half at 2R, move the stop to breakeven, exit the rest on the first red-bar close or at end of the 11:30 window); (d) the **2:1 minimum reward/risk** gate; (e) the **9:30-11:30 ET** window.
- [Inference] These parts are not mechanical or not reproducible from bars: Level 2 and time-and-sales exits, "extension bar" selling, catalyst quality, halts, and 10-second micro pullbacks. The rule "stop out minus 20 cents" if the structural stop is wider is mechanical but price-scale-specific: it only makes sense for $2-$20 stocks.
- [Inference] The criteria have drifted from float <100M and RVOL ≥2x on 2014-era pages to float <20M and RVOL ≥5x on newer pages. A backtest should name which version it is testing.
- [Inference] Cameron's 69% accuracy with ≥2:1 targets would be a very strong edge if both held at once. But no average-win/average-loss figure is published next to the accuracy figure, and the 2:1 is a stated target, not a measured realized ratio.

### Gaps
- I found no primary-source number for Cameron's **max daily loss** (the rule exists; the amount does not appear), and no published **realized average-win/average-loss (P/L ratio)** to pair with the 69% accuracy figure.
- The detailed Gap and Go entry setups 4-6 and the "Micro Pullback Strategy PDF" are behind a paywall or email gate. The micro-pullback page is a lead-generation page with no rules text. I did not read his book *How to Day Trade: The Plain Truth*.
- I could not date the watch-list page's "5 criteria" text, because the page carries no datePublished stamp.

## Q2. Ross Cameron: what he has published about his own results, what has been independently verified, and the FTC action

### Takeaway
Cameron publishes a CPA **examination** report (SingerLewak LLP, dated March 17, 2026). It covers realized gains in listed brokerage accounts from 2017 to 2025: **$18,810,638** of total realized gains from a $583.15 start, with large yearly variation ($309,884 in 2023, $6,200,647 in 2025). The figures exclude brokerage fees, count realized P&L only, and are restricted to Cameron's own use. Separately, the **FTC sued Warrior Trading and Cameron in April 2022**. It alleged deceptive earnings claims, and alleged that customers mostly lost money (simulator data: 74% of accounts lost money). The case was resolved by a stipulated order with a **$3,000,000 judgment** and a ban on unsubstantiated earnings claims. Defendants neither admitted nor denied the allegations. Over $2.9M was refunded to 20,402 people. I found no SEC, state attorney general, or class action filings.

## Q4. Independent and academic evidence on ORB, VWAP and intraday-momentum strategies, small-cap gap behavior, and retail day-trader profitability

### Takeaway
The research ORB/VWAP/noise-area results come from papers co-authored by Aziz with Zarattini and Barbon. They are strong on paper, but all are backtests that assume zero or minimal slippage:
- **QQQ ORB:** 675% total, Sharpe 1.12, 24% win rate.
- **Stocks-in-Play ORB:** >1,600%, Sharpe 2.81.
- **VWAP on QQQ:** 671%, Sharpe 2.1.
- **SPY noise-area momentum:** 1,985%, Sharpe 1.33.

Independent replications are mixed:
- The QQQ ORB **replicates** (Sharpe 1.06 vs 1.12) but its edge **breaks even at about 2.2¢/share of slippage**, and much of the P&L is from 2022.
- The SPY noise-area strategy replicates (Sharpe 1.11, 2020-2026), but its edge has been **~0 since 2025**.

Broader evidence:
- Market-level intraday momentum (Gao et al., JFE 2018) gives an academic basis for SPY trend-following.
- High-attention, overnight-gapping stocks tend to **reverse intraday** (Berkman et al. 2012; Concretum 2026). That is a headwind for buying small-cap gappers at the open.
- Most retail day traders lose money: 97% of persistent Brazilian day traders lost money; under 1% of Taiwanese day traders are predictably profitable; the FTC found 74% of Warrior Trading simulator accounts lost money.

### Cited Findings

**Zarattini & Aziz (2023), "Can Day Trading Really Be Profitable?" (SSRN 4416622; first published Apr 10, 2023; SSRN lists a revision of Apr 21, 2025)**
- [Academic/author] Rules:
  - QQQ, 5-minute bars, Jan 1, 2016 – Feb 17, 2023.
  - "if during the first 5 minutes the market moved up, we took a bullish position starting from the second candle's opening price… if the first 5-minute candle was negative, we took a bearish position… No positions were opened when the first 5-minute candle was a doji."

  - Rules as implemented: bands built from "the typical move of the last 14 days at each minute of the session, anchored to max/min(Open, previous Close)"; "A breakout at a 30-minute check → trend-following with a trailing stop on VWAP/bands, forced flat at 16:00. Vol targeting 2%/day, max leverage 4×."
  - Results (SPY via Alpaca, 2020-2026): Sharpe 1.11, alpha +16.7%/yr (t = 2.85), +2.6 bps/trade, win rate 41%, payoff 1.69. Year 2022: +25.8% while SPY was −19.5%.
  - "recent Sharpe ≈ 0 on both instruments" (SPY and ES) since 2025.
  - A 2025 follow-up (Maróy) claimed Sharpe >3 from parameter optimization. Under a pre-registered protocol, the replicator found "the paper's original configuration" was the in-sample winner, and quarterly walk-forward re-selection cut Sharpe to 0.57 vs 0.92.

  — [giovannibrusco/zarattini-2024-momentum-spy](https://github.com/giovannibrusco/zarattini-2024-momentum-spy)

**Other academic evidence on ORB and intraday momentum**
- [Academic] Gao, Han, Li & Zhou (2018), *JFE* 129: using SPY 1993-2013, the return from the prior close to 10:00 ET predicts the 15:30-16:00 return. The effect is stronger on high-volatility, high-volume, recession and macro-news days, and also appears in ten other heavily traded ETFs. — [WUSTL profile, "Market intraday momentum"](https://profiles.wustl.edu/en/publications/market-intraday-momentum/)
- [Academic] Holmberg, Lönnbark & Lundström (2013), *Finance Research Letters* 10(1): an ORB strategy based on the joint distribution of Open/High/Low/Close gives "significantly higher returns than zero" and "an increased success rate in relation to a fair game" (keywords include crude oil futures). — [IDEAS/RePEc](https://ideas.repec.org/a/eee/finlet/v10y2013i1p27-33.html)

**Evidence on small-cap and high-attention gap stocks' intraday behavior**
- [Academic] Berkman, Koch, Tuttle & Zhang (2012), *JFQA* 47(4): overnight returns tend to be positive and are followed by intraday reversals. The effect is concentrated in stocks that recently drew retail attention, is stronger for hard-to-value and costly-to-arbitrage stocks, and grows with retail sentiment. The implicit cost of buying these stocks at the open often exceeds the effective half-spread. The sample is the 3,000 largest U.S. stocks, 1996-2008, with roughly +10 bp/day overnight and −7 bp/day intraday reversal (per the paper's introduction, as summarized). — [Cambridge/JFQA](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/journal-of-financial-and-quantitative-analysis/article/paying-attention-overnight-returns-and-the-hidden-cost-of-buying-at-the-open/F9AAD159B512C651F09D5D52011D88E0); [KU repository PDF](https://kuscholarworks.ku.edu/bitstream/1808/10965/1/Koch_Paying%20Attention.pdf)
- [Quant firm research, paywalled, gross of costs] "Identifying Stocks to Fade" (May 23, 2026): in U.S. stocks from Jan 2012 to mid-May 2026, abnormally large overnight gaps tend to revert during the next session. A fade backtest showed CAGR ≈98%, Sharpe ≈2.08 and MDD ≈67% *gross*. The equity curve peaked in H1 2022, and the drawdown continued into 2026 (the authors suggest crowding). Borrow availability and cost are major frictions. — [Concretum Group Substack](https://concretumgroup.substack.com/p/identifying-stocks-to-fade)
- [Academic] Barber, Huang, Odean & Schwarz, "Attention-Induced Trading and Returns: Evidence from Robinhood Users." Intense Robinhood herding is followed by negative abnormal returns. One version reports 20-day abnormal returns of −4.7% for the top stocks bought and −19.6% for extreme herding events; the 2020 draft reports −3% / −6% over 5 days. — [SSRN draft PDF mirror](https://www.smallake.kr/wp-content/uploads/2020/12/SSRN-id3715077.pdf)

**Retail day-trader profitability**
- [Academic] Chague, De-Losso & Giovannetti, "Day Trading for a Living?" (Brazil equity futures, everyone who started in 2013-2015): of those who day traded ≥300 days, 97% lost money, only 0.4% earned more than a bank teller (US$54/day), and the authors found no evidence of learning. — [FGV repository](https://repositorio.fgv.br/items/e87d04fc-4b4c-4a56-ab5a-1c84c1afde1b/full)
- [Academic, as cited by Zarattini & Aziz] Barber et al. (Taiwan, 1992-2006): "less than 1% of those day traders were able to predictably and reliably earn positive abnormal returns net of fees." — [Zarattini & Aziz 2023 PDF](https://static1.squarespace.com/static/5983d931579fb366729580d8/t/643ed6765176b45506e41a01/1681839734183/SSRN-id4416622.pdf)
- [Academic, as cited in Warrior's own disclaimer] Jordan & Diltz (2003, *FAJ*): of 334 U.S. day-trading accounts (1998-99), "only 35% were profitable and only 14% generated profits in excess of $10,000." Garvey & Murphy (2005): of 1,146 accounts (Mar-Jun 2000), "only 50% were profitable." — [Warrior Trading disclaimer (Gap and Go page)](https://www.warriortrading.com/gap-go/)
- [Regulator] Warrior Trading's own customers: simulator accounts (Nov 2020 – Mar 2021) "74%… lost money, and only 10% earned more than $90." Broker records (May 2018 – May 2021) show "the vast majority of customers made no money or lost money." — [FTC Complaint ¶¶82, 85](https://www.ftc.gov/system/files/ftc_gov/pdf/2023198WarriorTradingComplaint_0.pdf)

### Inferences
- [Inference] The QQQ ORB is effectively "trade the direction of the first 5-minute bar with a tight stop and hold to the close." The low win rate (~24%) and long right tail mean results depend on a few trend days and volatile regimes (2020, 2022). Execution costs of a cent or two per share can erase the edge, so any backtest must model the spread and stop slippage, and must resolve intrabar stop-vs-target ambiguity conservatively.
- [Inference] All four headline studies share an author (Aziz) who sells day-trading education, as does the replication note. They also share design choices: leverage up to 4x, leveraged ETFs, and zero or near-zero slippage. Out-of-sample evidence after the papers' samples (2023-2026) is weak or negative in the independent replications: SPY noise-area Sharpe ≈0 since 2025, and QQQ ORB with 2023 decay hinted.
- [Inference] The small-cap gap evidence (Berkman et al.; Concretum fade; Robinhood herding) runs *against* naive long entries in high-attention gappers at the open, which is the Gap-and-Go universe. Cameron's model depends on catching the minority of continuation days with very tight stops and fast exits. That cannot be validated without small-cap tick or 1-minute data, and it carries halt, spread and borrow risks that 5-minute ETF bars do not show.

### Gaps
- I could not access the full SSRN PDFs of the Stocks-in-Play paper, the VWAP paper, or Beat the Market (SSRN returned 403). Exact rule text for the VWAP paper (bar size, flip timing) and the noise-area stop and evaluation cadence are taken from author decks, summaries and the replication. A QuantConnect user backtest of the VWAP paper reportedly produced lower figures; I could not verify it.
- I found no peer-reviewed study that directly tests Cameron-style low-float (<20M), $2-$20, ≥10% gap, ≥5x RVOL continuation entries net of costs.

## Q5. Which rules translate to QQQ/SPY/mega-cap 5-minute bars, and which need small-cap, low-float, news-driven stocks?

### Takeaway
Rules that are **directly testable on 5-minute bars of QQQ, SPY or mega-caps**:
- the Zarattini-Aziz 5-min ORB (exact spec);
- the noise-area/VWAP-trailing intraday momentum (30-min checks fit 5-min bars);
- Aziz's VWAP trend/false-breakout, 9/20-EMA trend, ORB-with-VWAP-stop, red-to-green toward the prior close, and RSI-extreme reversal rules (with some operationalization);
- a mega-cap "Stocks in Play" ORB if you have a cross-section of names and first-5-min volume history.

Cameron's setups **depend on what this dataset lacks**: stocks up ≥10%, priced $2-$20, float <20M, RVOL ≥5x, with breaking news, plus pre-market highs, 1-minute/10-second entries, Level 2 exits and halts. Only their price-pattern skeleton (first bar to make a new high after a 2-3 bar pullback, stop at the pullback low, 2:1 partial, breakeven stop) can be ported, and doing so tests a different hypothesis.

### Cited Findings
- The QQQ ORB was designed on QQQ/TQQQ 5-minute bars, with all parameters specified (first-bar direction, doji filter, stop at first-bar extreme, 10R/EOD, 1% risk, 4x cap, $0.0005/sh). — [Zarattini & Aziz 2023 PDF](https://static1.squarespace.com/static/5983d931579fb366729580d8/t/643ed6765176b45506e41a01/1681839734183/SSRN-id4416622.pdf)
- The independent replication used QQQ 5-minute bars and modeled $0.02/share entry and $0.04 stop slippage. — [giovannibrusco/zarattini-2023-orb-qqq](https://github.com/giovannibrusco/zarattini-2023-orb-qqq)
- The noise-area strategy checks signals only at HH:00/HH:30, uses 14-day bands and VWAP trailing stops, and is flat at 16:00. The authors' deck reports similar results on QQQ and on single mega-caps such as TSLA, NVDA, AAPL and AMD, with weaker Sharpe for AAPL (0.77) and NVDA (1.09). — [MoneyShow deck](https://bearbulltraders.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/Money_Show_Final.pdf); [replication README](https://github.com/giovannibrusco/zarattini-2024-momentum-spy)
- The Stocks-in-Play ORB filters need opening-range relative volume vs 14-day history across a broad universe, with price > $5, ADV ≥ 1M and ATR > $0.50. — [CXO Advisory](https://www.cxoadvisory.com/individual-investing/intraday-trading-of-overactive-stocks-via-opening-range-breakout/); [QuantConnect](https://www.quantconnect.com/research/18444/opening-range-breakout-for-stocks-in-play/)
- Aziz assigns large-float stocks "mostly Moving Average and Reversal" and medium floats "mostly VWAP and Support or Resistance" ([BBT figures PDF](https://bearbulltraders.com/docs/AndrewAziz-How_to_Day_Trade_for_a_Living_AUDIOBOOK-FIGS.pdf)). His 2023 ORB playbook is "Only mid-floats: +$10 price range. No low floats" ([BBT ORB deck](https://bearbulltraders.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/04/5-Min-ORB-Strategy-ORB.pdf)).
- Cameron's filters are price under $20 (sweet spot $2-8), float under 20M, RVOL ≥5x, up ≥10%, and a news catalyst ([Warrior watch-list](https://www.warriortrading.com/day-trading-watch-list-top-stocks-to-watch/)). His entries use pre-market highs and the first 1-minute candle ([Warrior Gap and Go](https://www.warriortrading.com/gap-go/)), and micro pullbacks on "10-second or 1-minute charts" ([Warrior patterns](https://www.warriortrading.com/best-day-trading-patterns/)). Exits use Level 2 and time-and-sales ([Warrior Bull Flag](https://www.warriortrading.com/bull-flag-trading/)).

### Inferences
- [Inference] **Directly backtestable on 5-min QQQ/SPY (exact or near-exact):**
  1. Zarattini-Aziz 5-min ORB. Signal on the 09:30 bar; enter at the 09:35 open; stop at the 09:30 bar's low/high; 10R target or EOD exit; skip dojis (define a threshold, e.g. |close−open| < x% of range). Add realistic slippage (the replication's break-even is ~2.2¢/sh).
  2. Noise-area momentum. Compute σ(t) from |close(t)/open − 1| averaged over 14 days at each 5-min timestamp. Bands: max(Open, prior Close)×(1+σ) and min(Open, prior Close)×(1−σ). Check at HH:00/HH:30. Trailing stop at max(upper band, VWAP) for longs (min(...) for shorts); flat at 16:00. Vol-target sizing with a 4x cap. *The exact stop formula comes from the replication README and should be confirmed against the paper.*
  3. VWAP trend (Zarattini-Aziz): long above / short below VWAP on bar closes. On 5-min bars this approximates the paper, whose bar size I could not confirm.
- [Inference] **Translatable with operational choices (Aziz discretionary rules):**
  - ORB-5/15/30 on the bar *body* range, only if range ≪ 14-day ATR (pick a cutoff, e.g. <25-50% of ATR). Stop on a 5-min close beyond VWAP; target = prior-day close/high/low or a daily MA; window up to 1 hour after the open.
  - VWAP false-breakout (10:00-10:30) and VWAP hold/loss trend (after 10:30).
  - 9/20-EMA trend mid-day: enter near the MA, exit on a close through the MA.
  - Red-to-green: trade toward the prior close when volume rises.
  - Reversal: ≥4-5 consecutive same-color 5-min bars plus RSI >90/<10 (RSI period unspecified; 14 is a common default). On SPY/QQQ such extremes will be rare, so the sample will be small.
  - Mega-cap "stocks in play": rank the ~50-500 largest names by first-5-min RVOL vs their 14-day average and trade the top N with ORB rules. This needs per-stock 5-min volume history and is a large-cap approximation of the paper's 7,000-stock universe.
- [Inference] **Not testable on this dataset (small-cap/news dependent):**
  - Cameron's stock filter: SPY/QQQ never meet "up ≥10% with RVOL ≥5x," and mega-caps fail both the $2-$20 price and <20M float filters.
  - Pre-market-high breaks: these need extended-hours bars.
  - The 1-minute first-candle ORB and 10-second micro pullbacks: these need sub-5-minute bars.
  - Level 2 and tape exits, halts (LULD), short-sale restriction, and whole/half-dollar magnet effects on $2-$10 stocks.
  - A 5-min-bar version of "first candle to make a new high after 2-3 red candles, stop at pullback low, sell ½ at 2R, breakeven stop, exit on first red 5-min close" *can* be coded on mega-caps or QQQ. But it tests generic pullback-continuation, not Cameron's edge claim, which he attributes to RVOL ≥5x low-float names (FTC ¶42 quote).
- [Inference] **Bar-resolution caveats for 5-minute backtests:**
  - Stops are tight: Cameron's ≤$0.20, Aziz's 5-10¢ below the MA, the ATR-stop ORB variant at 5% of ATR. Stop and target can both fall inside one 5-min bar, so assume stop-first.
  - VWAP should be computed from intrabar volume (typical price × volume per 5-min bar is an approximation).
  - The Stocks-in-Play paper's ORB uses stop orders at the range extremes, which fill intrabar and are subject to slippage on fast opens.

### Gaps
- I could not confirm the bar resolution of the original VWAP paper, or whether the noise-area exits are checked only at 30-min marks or continuously. Both affect 5-min fidelity.
- I found no published test of Cameron-style bull-flag/first-new-high rules on large-cap 5-minute data, and no test of Aziz's VWAP-stop ORB variant against the research ORB.
